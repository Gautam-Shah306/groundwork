# Kora Living — Customer Support Policies

> **Note:** Kora Living is a **fictional** D2C home-goods retailer created for the Groundwork synthetic demonstration workspace. All policies below are fictional and exist solely to ground policy-based draft replies.

---

## 1. Returns

- **Return window:** Customers may request a return within **7 calendar days from the delivery date**.
- **Item condition requirements:** Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags, inserts, and protective materials intact.
- **Non-returnable items:** Custom-made orders, monogrammed textiles, and items marked final sale / clearance cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.
- **Return pickup:** Once a return request is verified, a reverse-pickup is scheduled with the courier partner. If reverse-pickup is unavailable for a pin code, the team provides self-ship instructions.

## 2. Replacements for Damaged or Wrong Items

- **Reporting window:** Damaged, defective, or wrong items must be reported within **48 hours of delivery**.
- **Required evidence:** Customers must share clear photographs of:
  1. The damaged or incorrect product.
  2. The outer shipping box and visible shipping label.
- **Resolution:** After photo verification, Kora Living issues a free replacement (subject to stock availability) or a full refund to the original payment method at no extra shipping cost.

## 3. Refund Processing Time

- **Inspection and initiation:** Refunds for returned items are initiated once the returned parcel reaches the warehouse and passes a basic condition check.
- **Timeline:** Refunds take **5–7 business days** after the returned item is received to appear on the **original payment method** (card, UPI, net banking, or wallet).
- **Cash on delivery (COD):** For COD orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer or UPI after the customer verifies payout details through the secure link.

## 4. Double-Charge and Failed-Order Payment Reversals

- **Automatic reversal:** If a customer is charged twice or payment is deducted while the order failed to confirm, the payment gateway initiates an automatic reversal within **5–7 business days**.
- **Escalation threshold:** If the reversed amount has not reflected in the customer's bank or card statement after **7 business days**, escalate the case to the **Payments** team with the transaction reference (`[TRANSACTION_ID]`) and payment date (`[PAYMENT_DATE]`) for gateway reconciliation.

## 5. Delivery Timelines

- **Metro cities:** **3–5 business days** from dispatch.
- **Non-metro and other locations:** **5–8 business days** from dispatch.
- **Tracking delays:** Carrier tracking updates may lag by up to 24 hours after dispatch. If tracking has not moved for more than 3 days past the expected window, the Order support team opens a courier trace.

## 6. Address Changes

- **Before dispatch:** Shipping address edits and phone number updates are permitted **only before the order is dispatched** from the warehouse.
- **After dispatch:** Once dispatched, Kora Living cannot guarantee courier rerouting. Customers may coordinate delivery attempts with the carrier once tracking is active, or allow the parcel to return to origin for re-shipment or refund.

## 7. Account Lockouts and Login Issues

- **First step:** Direct customers to the **self-service password reset** link on the sign-in page using their registered email or phone number.
- **Temporary lockout:** After repeated unsuccessful login attempts, accounts lock temporarily for **15 minutes** before another attempt succeeds.
- **Escalation:** If the reset link does not arrive (after checking spam/junk folders) or the account remains inaccessible after 15 minutes, route to support for manual account verification.

## 8. Escalation Rules

- **Mandatory Escalations queue (`Escalations`):**
  - Any ticket containing a **legal threat**, consumer-court complaint, regulatory notice, or **bank chargeback threat** must be routed immediately to the **Escalations** team lead at **High** priority.
  - Any exception request outside stated policy windows (for example, returns past 7 days, damage reports past 48 hours, or unresolved refunds/reversals past 7 business days) requires **team lead approval**.
- **No unauthorised exceptions:** Frontline agents and draft replies must never promise fee waivers, out-of-window refunds, or compensation credits without recorded lead approval.

## 9. Tone and Drafting Guidelines

- **Voice:** Empathetic, calm, respectful, and concise. Acknowledge frustration plainly without defensiveness.
- **Policy fidelity:** Quote only verified Kora Living policy timelines and requirements from this document. Never make promises beyond policy.
- **No invented facts:** Never fabricate order IDs, tracking states, courier names, delivery dates, refund amounts, or warehouse confirmations.
- **Bracketed placeholders:** Whenever a customer-specific detail is not present in the ticket text, insert an explicit bracketed placeholder, such as:
  - `[ORDER_ID]`
  - `[TRACKING_STATUS]`
  - `[DELIVERY_DATE]`
  - `[REFUND_AMOUNT]`
  - `[TRANSACTION_ID]`
  - `[CUSTOMER_NAME]`
- **Human review required:** Draft responses are proposals for operator review only and are never sent directly to customers.
